01

Sunlit Storybook

“The cozy picture-book, lovingly painted.”

A premium evolution of what Orenj already is. Think a hand-painted children's book crossed with Studio Ghibli warmth: gouache gradients, soft rim-light, paper grain, golden-hour skies. Pip gains real shading and a glow. Nothing is jarring — it's the current app, matured into something you'd want to frame. Lowest risk, highest coziness.

02

Cosmic Citrus

“Electric, glowing, dopamine on tap.”

Bold and energetic. Deep-space navy, neon citrus, holographic gradients, bloom and glass. Pip becomes a little luminous sun-sprite trailing sparks. This leans hard into the game-y dopamine — rings pulse, buttons glow, particles drift. It reads modern, premium, and exciting — closer to a flagship mobile game than an app. Highest energy, most distinctive screenshot.

03

Paper Citrus Co.

“Hand-cut paper, stacked into a tiny world.”

A tactile cut-paper / risograph world. Everything looks layered from colored construction paper with soft drop-shadows and a printed grain, like a diorama you could touch. It's the most design-forward and ownable look — calm, warm, and distinctive, in the spirit of boutique apps (Headspace, indie puzzle games). Most unique brand, most "crafted by hand."
